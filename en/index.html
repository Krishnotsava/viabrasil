Our Journey

Official Partner for Global Brands in the Region

Founded in 1993 by businessman David Jacob (commencing retail activities in the 1980s), Via Brasil has established itself as the leading importer and distributor of luxury and niche perfumery in Paraguay.

Headquartered in the prestigious Via Brasil Building in the center of Ciudad del Este, we operate state-of-the-art temperature-controlled storage, guaranteeing the chemical integrity of the finest olfactive creations.

Our model is strictly Mayorista and institutional: we represent brands at trade fairs, educate the specialized market, and guide the final consumer to authorized retail channels.

Niche Fragrances

Niche perfumery represents the pinnacle of artistic expression in fragrance. These brands work outside the mass marketing circuit, focusing on the absolute quality of ingredients, the perfumer's creative freedom, and striking narrative concepts.

Our Mayorista commercial approach: We provide official representation and support for boutique stores, premium perfumeries, and luxury spaces in shopping centers. We do not sell retail directly or digitally; each interested party is directed to regional authorized retailers' channels.

Key portfolio niche brands:

  • Boadicea
  • Coreterno
  • Birkholz
  • Simone Andreoli
  • Spirit of Kings

Luxury Perfumery

Luxury perfumery hosts iconic brands of global prestige. Combining haute couture, sophisticated bottle designs, and worldwide recognition, these fragrances form the core pillar of the prestige market and enjoy strong demand from a select public.

Our Mayorista commercial approach: We guarantee a continuous flow of authentic imports, full customs clearance with legal security, and large-scale regional distribution with exclusive commercial conditions.

Featured luxury and distribution brands:

  • Nusuk
  • Amaran
  • Emirates Pride
  • Riiffs
  • The Merchant of Venice
